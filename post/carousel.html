Four folders.

reels/

├─ refs/    reels you love
├─ assets/  logo, fonts, music

Open the folder in Claude Code. It installs ffmpeg + Whisper for you on the first run.

Prompt 01Kill the retakes
1

Cut every
bad take.

> prompt
Transcribe everything in raw/. Where I repeat a line, keep only the last take. Remove silences over 0.4s and filler words. Stitch it into out/cut.mp4.

20 minutes of fumbling → a tight 45-second cut.

Prompt 02Captions
2

Captions that
actually pop.

> prompt
Add word-by-word captions to out/cut.mp4. Bold sans, 3 words max per line, centered in the lower third. Highlight the key word in orange.
